# Claude Impact Lab: Lead Finder for Small Businesses

Small business owners know who their best customers are, but finding new ones takes hours: searching maps, digging through websites for an email, guessing who to contact first. This project does that work for them.

Built at the Claude Impact Lab Hackathon, Seattle, October 25, 2026.

## What’s in this repo

| Part | What it does | Folder |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead Finder** | Answer six questions. It searches Google Maps, finds emails on each business’s website, reads About pages for the owner’s name, and ranks who to contact first, with a first email ready. [Open the app](PASTE_STREAMLIT_LINK_HERE) | [`lead-finder/`](lead-finder/) |
| **Lead Ranker** | Already have a list? Paste it in any format and get it ranked, with emails drafted and a do-not-contact list. | [`lead-ranker/`](lead-ranker/) |

## What makes a good lead

A real, open business (on Google Maps, not marked closed), that’s active (recent Google reviews), with a valid email (found on its own website, on a domain that can receive mail), where you know who to ask for (the owner’s name from their About page), and that matches the kind of customer you described. Details in the [Lead Finder README](lead-finder/README.md).
